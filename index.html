Since 1926

A hundred years, one oven at a time

Tamizhan Bakery began as a single family-run oven in the lanes of old Madras. Four generations later, we still bake with the same jaggery-and-butter instincts — just with a faster delivery bike.

1926

A single oven in Georgetown

Our founders started baking bread and rusks for the neighbourhood, using recipes carried over from home kitchens.

1958

The first celebration cake

Tamizhan Bakery baked its first fresh cream birthday cake — the Pineapple Fresh Cream recipe we still sell today.

1991

Hygiene-first kitchens

We modernised our kitchens with standardised hygiene protocols, without changing a single family recipe.

2016

Chennai-wide delivery

We expanded from our Georgetown storefront to serve every zone across Chennai.

2024

2-Hour Express Delivery

A dedicated fleet now gets a fresh cake to your door in under two hours, anywhere in the city.
